Design constraints (from [requirements](../docs/requirements.md)):

- Closed with M3 screws into heat-set inserts; no snap-fits.
- Card recess in the lid sized for the cards, with the RC522 antenna ≤ 3 mm
  below it.
- Translucent dome, ≥ 2 mm wall, over a printed cylinder carrying the 12-LED
  strip segment (~64 mm diameter for 20 cm of strip).
- Speaker grille with holes too small for fingers.
- USB-C input recessed in the back wall; cable strain relief.
- Large lamp button and volume knob reachable by a 3-year-old.
